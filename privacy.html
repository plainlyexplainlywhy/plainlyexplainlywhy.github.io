Privacy Policy — Content Hub

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who we are

Content Hub is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel “Plainly Explainly Why”. It is used only by the channel owner to manage that channel. Contact: plainlyexplainedwhy@gmail.com.

What data Content Hub accesses

When the channel owner connects their Google account, Content Hub uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publishing schedules to the owner's own channel;
  • read the status of those uploads;
  • read statistics of the owner's own channel and videos (such as views, watch time and subscriber changes) through the YouTube Analytics API.

Content Hub does not access data of other YouTube users, viewers or channels, other than public information returned by the YouTube Data API.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and channel statistics are stored on the owner's private server, which is not publicly reachable. Tokens are stored encrypted. Data is kept only as long as needed to operate the channel and is deleted when the connection is removed.

Sharing

Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Content Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

Content Hub uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops Content Hub from uploading or reading data; stored tokens are then deleted.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
