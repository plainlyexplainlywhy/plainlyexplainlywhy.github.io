Plainly Explainly Why

One question per video, answered with real sources and real data.

Plainly Explainly Why is an English-language YouTube channel that explains money, science and space, strange history and the background behind world news. Every key claim is checked against independent sources, and charts are built from official public data.

Content Hub

Content Hub is the private production tool behind this channel. It is used only by the channel owner to prepare videos, upload them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the official YouTube Data API, and read the channel's own statistics through the YouTube Analytics API. It is not offered to other users.
